RING RACERS WORLDWIDE -- THE DUB PACKS' CREDITS
===============================================

The dub packs in this folder give characters another set of voice lines,
chosen by each pilot at character select. They hold only sounds and DUBDEF
lumps, load on your machine alone, and change nothing in the race.

The voices belong to their owners and are used here without intent to
profit, as fan works. The lines were gathered and arranged by the people
below; Ring Racers Worldwide only converted them into dubs (loudness
evened, files renamed, DUBDEFs written).
