RING RACERS WORLDWIDE -- THE DUB PACKS' CREDITS
===============================================

The dub packs in this folder give characters another set of voice lines,
chosen by each pilot at character select. They hold only sounds and DUBDEF
lumps, load on your machine alone, and change nothing in the race.

The voices belong to their owners and are used here without intent to
profit, as fan works. The packs were made by Gibax; Ring Racers Worldwide
converted them into dubs (loudness evened, files renamed, DUBDEFs written).

JapDub_Worldwide.wad -- the game's own characters, in Japanese
  - From KL_JapDub_v1_RR.wad, Gibax's port to Ring Racers of ZaniteKnuckles'
    Japanese dub mod for SRB2Kart.
  - Fang: Kagura Mutsuki's voice (BlazBlue Centralfiction). Surge: Baiken's
    (Guilty Gear Strive). Jet: his own Japanese lines (Sonic Riders). Arle:
    her own Japanese lines (Puyo Puyo). Gathered by Gibax.

JapDub_Addons_Worldwide.wad -- addon characters, in Japanese
  - From KL_JapDub_v1_RR.wad (above): Gamma, Miku, Omega, SA2 Sonic, Super
    Sonic, Ulala, Vector, Vyse -- heard once each addon is loaded.
  - Ragna: his own Japanese lines (BlazBlue), gathered by Gibax.
  - CD Sonic: the SegaSonic lines below, from KL_SegaSonic.pk3.
  - Sonic Beat: the ComoDub lines below, from
    CKV_Super_Sonic_Japanese_Voice.pk3.

SegaSonic_Worldwide.wad -- Sonic, "SegaSonic"
  - From KL_SegaSonic.pk3, by Gibax.

ComoDub_Worldwide.wad and ComoDub_Addons_Worldwide.wad -- Sonic, and the
addons' Super Sonic, "ComoDub"
  - From CKV_Super_Sonic_Japanese_Voice.pk3, an SRB2Kart addon by Gibax.

Dr. Robotnik's Ring Racers is by Kart Krew Dev. Sonic the Hedgehog and its
characters belong to SEGA. Neither is affiliated with these packs.
